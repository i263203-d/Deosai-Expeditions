Chitral & Kalash

Home to the indigenous Kalash valleys of Bumburet, Rumbur, and Birir, the towering 7,708m summit of Tirich Mir, and the world's highest polo field at Shandur Pass.

Primary Access
Lowari Tunnel (N-45)
Key Festivals
Chilam Joshi • Uchal
Shandur Pass
3,700m Polo Ground
Highest Peak
Tirich Mir (7,708m)

Living Heritage of the Hindu Kush

Separated from the rest of Pakistan by the dramatic Lowari Tunnel and linked to Gilgit-Baltistan via the Shandur Pass, Chitral offers a cultural and alpine experience unlike anywhere else in South Asia. The three Kalash valleys preserve centuries-old wooden architecture, vibrant embroidered dress, and seasonal thanksgiving festivals.

Kalash Valleys (Bumburet, Rumbur, Birir)

Explore cedar-fringed villages, traditional wooden homes, and the Kalashadur Museum with respectful local cultural guides.

Chilam Joshi & Uchal Festivals

Celebrate the arrival of spring in mid-May and the summer harvest in August with traditional Kalash folk music and dance.

Shandur Polo Pass (3,700m)

Known as the Roof of the World, hosting the annual freestyle polo match between Chitral and Gilgit every July.

Chitral Fort & Garam Chashma

Visit the historic riverside Chitral Fort, Shahi Mosque, and natural mineral hot springs tucked near the Afghan border.

Overland Circuit to Phander & Gilgit

From June through October, travelers can cross the scenic Shandur Pass directly from Chitral into Ghizer's turquoise Phander Valley and onward to Gilgit and Hunza—creating Pakistan's ultimate east-to-west mountain loop.
