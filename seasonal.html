FOUR SEASONS IN THE NORTH

Seasonal Guide

Every season transforms Pakistan's northern valleys. From spring cherry blossoms and Kalash festivals to summer Deosai wildflowers, golden autumn foliage, and winter ski slopes.

Spring (March – May)
8°C – 18°C Hunza, Skardu & Kalash

Cherry Blossom & Chilam Joshi

In April, snow-draped peaks frame millions of blooming pink apricot and white cherry blossoms across Altit, Ganish, and Nagar. Mid-May brings the famous Kalash Chilam Joshi spring festival in Chitral.

Summer (June – August)
12°C – 26°C Deosai, Fairy Meadows, K2 & Shandur

High Passes & Wildflower Bloom

All high mountain passes—Babusar, Khunjerab, Shandur, and Deosai Plains—open for summer crossings. Ideal for K2 Base Camp treks, Sheosar Lake camping, and escaping city heat.

Autumn (September – November)
4°C – 16°C Hunza, Ghizer & Skardu

Golden Foliage Expedition

October transforms the northern valleys into a world-famous photographer's paradise. Poplar, birch, and apricot trees glow with golden-amber tones beneath crisp sapphire skies.

Winter (December – February)
-6°C – 8°C Malam Jabba, Swat & Lower Hunza

Skiing & Snowbound Valleys

Experience fresh powder skiing at Malam Jabba in Swat, serene frozen Attabad Lake viewpoints, and snow-covered Katpana desert dunes accessible via all-weather flights to Skardu.
