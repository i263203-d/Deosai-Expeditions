FIELD ESSENTIALS & SAFETY

Travel Tips & Safety

Everything you need to know before you travel: visa regulations, NOC permits, mobile connectivity, local customs, essential packing gear, and high-altitude safety.

Pakistan Online e-Visa

Citizens of over 190 countries can obtain an official Pakistan Tourist e-Visa online within 48 to 72 hours via the official NADRA visa portal (visa.nadra.gov.pk). DEOSAI Expeditions provides full Letter of Invitation (LOI) documentation.

NOC & Restricted Areas

Good news: The Government of Pakistan has abolished NOC requirements for standard tourist regions including Hunza, Skardu, Swat, Chitral, and Fairy Meadows. Only technical mountaineering expeditions above 6,000m require official clearance.

SCOM SIM Card (Telecom)

While Jazz and Zong work in major hubs, the Special Communications Organization (SCOM) is the primary mobile network with 4G coverage throughout Gilgit-Baltistan. We assist you in acquiring tourist SCOM SIM cards upon arrival in Gilgit/Skardu.

Currency & ATMs

The Pakistani Rupee (PKR) is universally used. While luxury hotels (Serena/Luxus) accept Visa and MasterCard, remote bazaars, tea houses, and jeep drivers operate purely on cash. ATMs are available in Islamabad, Gilgit, Chitral, and Skardu town.

Altitude Health (AMS)

Drink 3 to 4 liters of water daily. Avoid rapid ascents without acclimatization nights. Consult your physician regarding Acetazolamide (Diamox) for high-altitude passes such as Babusar (4,173m) and Deosai (4,114m).

Packing & Dress Etiquette

Pack thermal layers, a windproof shell, SPF 50+ sunscreen, UV sunglasses, and a 20,000 mAh power bank. Out of respect for local hospitality, wear modest clothing covering shoulders and knees and ask before photographing locals.
